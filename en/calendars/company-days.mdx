---
title: "Company days"
description: "Add additional company days off or company working days for the whole organization or one work location."
---

**Who:** HR, Administrator, Owner.
**Where:** **Settings → Work time → Company days**, lists **Additional company days off** and **Company working days**.

## Additional company days off

Days off beyond public holidays, for example a day off on Christmas Eve. They don't reduce anyone's leave balance and don't count towards request length.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a day">
    Under **Additional company days off**, click **Add day off**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the day">
    Enter the **Name** and choose the **Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the scope">
    In **Applies to**, choose **Entire organization** or **Specific work location**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A day off that has already passed can't be removed — it explains earlier requests.
</Note>

## Company working days

Extra **working** days, for example an adopted recommendation to transfer a working day, or a make-up day. Such a day counts in a request even when it's a rest day in the schedule.

Under **Company working days**, click **Add working day** and fill in the same fields.

You can't set a company working day on a public holiday or on a day that's already a company day off.
