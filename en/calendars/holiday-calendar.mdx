---
title: "Holiday calendar"
description: "Review the suggested holidays for a work location and year, correct them and confirm the calendar Tivero uses to count requests."
---

Tivero suggests holidays from its calendar provider. You review them for each work location and year, correct anything that differs for you, and confirm. Leave is calculated **only from a confirmed calendar**.

**Who:** HR, Administrator, Owner.
**Where:** **Settings → Working days and holidays**, section **Holiday calendar by work location**.

## Confirm the calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the work location and year">
    Choose the **Work location** and the **Year**. You confirm each work location and each year separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the holidays">
    Check every date on the list. The **Verified by Tivero** or **Provider data** label tells you where the suggestion comes from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct it if needed">
    Use the actions next to a holiday (described below) if something differs for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm the … calendar** with the selected year. You confirm exactly the calendar shown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** the status changes to **Confirmed** with the confirmation date. From then on, people at this work location can submit requests for that year.

<Warning>
  In some countries, whether a holiday is a day off depends on contracts or collective agreements. Tivero shows a note when this applies. The provider's suggestion is not legal advice — your organization decides which calendar applies.
</Warning>

## Calendar statuses

| Status | What it means | Are requests counted? |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Your organization confirmed the calendar. | yes |
| **Needs confirmation** | There is a suggestion, but nobody has confirmed it yet. | no — requests are blocked |
| **No data** | Tivero doesn't have a calendar for this work location and year yet. | no — requests are blocked |

## Correct a holiday

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Treat as a normal scheduled day** | The holiday isn't a day off for you — it counts as a normal day according to the schedule. Undo it with **Restore holiday**. |
| **Move holiday** | The holiday becomes a normal day, and the date you choose (**Day off instead**) becomes a day off at this work location. Enter the **Name of the day off**. |

Your company days off and working days for the year show under **Your company days off and working days this year**. You add them in [Company days](/en/calendars/company-days).

## Update from the provider

If the provider changes a calendar you've already confirmed, Tivero shows a **A calendar update from the provider is available** card listing added and removed holidays.

- Until you accept it, leave is calculated from the calendar you confirmed.
- Click **Accept update** to apply it. Your corrections stay.

## Each person's calendar

Each person is counted against the holidays of their own work location. By default, that's the organization's work location. You change it on the person page — see [Work locations](/en/calendars/work-locations).
