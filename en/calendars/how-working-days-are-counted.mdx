---
title: "How working days are counted"
description: "What Tivero uses to work out a request's length: the work schedule, the confirmed holiday calendar, company days and statutory transfers."
---

An absence request is counted in working days, not calendar days. Tivero works out a person's working days from several settings your organization configures.

## What makes up a person's working-day calendar

| Part | What it does | Where you set it |
|---|---|---|
| **Work schedule** | Decides which weekdays are working days (for example Monday–Friday). | [Work schedules](/en/calendars/work-schedules) |
| **Work location** | Decides which holiday calendar applies to the person. | [Work locations](/en/calendars/work-locations) |
| **Confirmed holiday calendar** | Removes holidays from the count. Includes your corrections: holidays treated as normal days and moved holidays. | [Holiday calendar](/en/calendars/holiday-calendar) |
| **Company days** | Additional company days off and company working days. | [Company days](/en/calendars/company-days) |
| **Statutory transfers** | Statutory working-day transfers, if the organization applies them. | [Work schedules](/en/calendars/work-schedules) |

<Warning>
  Tivero counts requests **only from a holiday calendar your organization has confirmed**. If the calendar for a work location and year isn't confirmed, requests for that period are blocked with "The holiday calendar needs to be set up by an administrator.". This is a deliberate safeguard: Tivero doesn't guess which days are off for you.
</Warning>

## Who decides the holidays

Tivero suggests holidays from its calendar provider as a **reference calendar**. Your organization decides which days are off: it reviews the suggestion, corrects it and confirms it. Only the confirmed calendar is used to count requests.

Tivero doesn't guarantee that the suggestion is legally correct in every country. In some countries, whether a holiday is a day off depends on contracts or collective agreements. Check each date before you confirm.

## Check how a specific request was calculated

Open the request. The **Calculation details** section shows how many days count, the work location, the schedule, and every day that doesn't count, with the reason. Approvers see the same information under the **How was … calculated?** link.

Possible reasons:

| Reason | Counts towards the request? |
|---|---|
| **Weekend** | no |
| **Public holiday** | no |
| **Additional company day off** | no |
| **Rest day in the work schedule** | no |
| **Statutory rest day** | no |
| **Statutory working day** | yes |
| **Company working day** | yes |

## Changing settings

Changes to the schedule, work location or calendar apply from a chosen date. They don't recalculate requests that were already submitted.
