title: "How working days are counted"
description: "What Tivero uses to work out a request's length: the work schedule, the holiday calendar, company days and statutory transfers."
---

An absence request is counted in working days, not calendar days. Tivero works out a person's working days from several settings your organization configures.

## What makes up a person's working-day calendar

| Part | What it does | Where you set it |
|---|---|---|
| **Work schedule** | Decides which weekdays are working days (for example Monday–Friday). | [Work schedules](/en/calendars/work-schedules) |
| **Work location** | Decides which holiday calendar applies to the person. | [Work locations](/en/calendars/work-locations) |
| **Holiday calendar** | Removes holidays from the count. Includes your corrections: holidays treated as normal days and moved holidays. | [Holiday calendar](/en/calendars/holiday-calendar) |
| **Company days** | Additional company days off and company working days. | [Company days](/en/calendars/company-days) |
| **Statutory transfers** | Statutory working-day transfers, if the organization applies them. | [Work schedules](/en/calendars/work-schedules) |

<Warning>
  Tivero counts requests only from the **active holiday calendar** for the work location and year — it doesn't guess which days are off for you. If there is no such calendar yet, requests for that period are blocked with a message giving the reason (see [Holiday calendar needs setup](/en/troubleshooting/holiday-calendar-needs-setup)).
</Warning>

## Who decides the holidays

It depends on the calendar mode (see [Holiday calendar](/en/calendars/holiday-calendar)):

- **Managed by Tivero** — Tivero uses a calendar it has reviewed and keeps it up to date automatically. Your organization doesn't need to confirm it, but can correct individual holidays.
- **Confirmed by your organization** — Tivero suggests holidays from its calendar provider as a **reference calendar**. Your organization reviews the suggestion, corrects it and confirms it. Only the confirmed calendar is used to count requests.

Tivero doesn't guarantee that the calendar is legally correct in every country and situation. In some countries, whether a holiday is a day off depends on contracts or collective agreements. If a day is different for you, correct the holiday in the calendar.

## Check how a specific request was calculated

Open the request. The **Calculation details** section shows how many days count, the work location, the schedule, and every day that doesn't count, with the reason. Approvers see the same information under the **How was … calculated?** link.

Possible reasons:

| Reason | Counts towards the request? |
|---|---|
| **Weekend** | no |
| **Public holiday** | no |
| **Additional company day off** | no |
| **Rest day in the work schedule** | no |
| **Statutory rest day** | no |
| **Statutory working day** | yes |
| **Company working day** | yes |

## Changing settings

Changes to the schedule, work location or calendar apply from a chosen date. They don't recalculate requests that were already submitted — this also applies to updates of a calendar managed by Tivero.
