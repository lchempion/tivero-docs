## Data isolation

Each workspace's data is kept separate from every other workspace. Someone who belongs to several organizations sees only that organization's data in each one.

## Data export

HR, Administrators and the Owner can download requests, balances and people at any time in **Settings → Export**. See [Data export](/en/reports/exports). Export also works in read-only mode, for example after the trial or a subscription ends.

## Moving your data to another provider

**Who:** the Owner.
**Where:** **Settings**, section **Moving your data to another provider**.

If you're moving your data to another system, you can issue a credential for the destination provider. It only allows reading this workspace's exportable data through the switching interface and gives no access to the application.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Issue a credential">
    Enter a name (for example the destination provider), choose **Valid for** and click **Issue credential**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy it right away">
    The credential is shown only once. Copy it and pass it to the destination provider over a secure channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can **Revoke** the credential at any time. The list shows when it expires and when it was last used.

## Delete the organization

**Who:** the Owner.
**Where:** **Settings → Danger zone**.

<Warning>
  Deletion covers all people, requests, balances and settings. Access is cut off immediately. For **30 days** you can cancel the deletion; after that, data is erased permanently.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the deletion">
    Click **Delete organization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Type the organization name and click **Schedule deletion**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To cancel the deletion during the grace period, sign in — you see **Workspace scheduled for deletion** with a **Cancel deletion** button.

<Tip>
  Before deleting the organization, download a data export if you'll need the data. If you have an active subscription, also see [cancelling the subscription](/en/billing/subscription-and-payments).
</Tip>
