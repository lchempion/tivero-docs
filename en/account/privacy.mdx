## Analytics and marketing consent

On your first visit, Tivero asks for your consent. The product works exactly the same if you decline.

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Necessary** | The sign-in session and security. The product doesn't work without them, so they aren't a choice. |
| **Analytics** | Which screens are used and where people get stuck. We don't send names, email addresses, comments or URLs carrying identifiers. |
| **Marketing** | Applies only to the tivero.app website. The app loads no marketing tools. |

You can change your consent at any time: **Settings → Privacy → Privacy and analytics → Change preferences**.

## Health data and private types

- Details of private types (for example "Medical leave (L4)") are visible only to the person, their manager and HR. Everyone else sees "Absence".
- Don't enter a diagnosis or medical details in request comments.
- Tivero doesn't accept medical documents.

## Requests about your data (GDPR)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Settings → Privacy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the request type">
    **Export my data**, **Rectify my data** or **Delete my data**. Optionally add details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send request**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Data requests are handled by your organization's HR. Deletion is a request — the organization may be required to retain some records for legal and accounting reasons.
