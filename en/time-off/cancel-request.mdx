---
title: "Cancel a request"
description: "Cancel a pending or approved request and see what happens to the balance."
---

**Who:** the person who submitted the request, plus HR, Administrators and the Owner.
**What you can cancel:** requests with the **Pending** or **Approved** status. You can't cancel a declined or already cancelled request.

## Cancel a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    Go to **Requests** and open the request (your own are on the **Mine** tab).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Click **Cancel request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm in the **Cancel this request?** dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You can't undo a cancellation from this screen. If the absence is still needed, submit a new request.
</Warning>

## What happens after cancelling

| Request before cancelling | Result |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | The request leaves the review list. The approver no longer needs to decide. The balance doesn't change. |
| **Approved** | The days go back to the balance (if the type deducts from it). The balance history shows a **Cancelled request** entry. |

If your organization syncs calendars with Outlook, the event for the approved request is removed from the calendar. See [Outlook calendar](/en/microsoft-365/outlook-calendar).
