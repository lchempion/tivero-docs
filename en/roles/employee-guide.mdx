This guide is for everyone who submits absence requests. Detailed instructions are in the linked articles.

## What you do in Tivero

- Check your balance.
- Submit absence requests and follow their status.
- See who on your team is away.
- Manage your details and notification preferences.

## First steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation and sign in">
    Use the email address the invitation was sent to. See the [Employee quick start](/en/getting-started/employee-quick-start).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Dashboard">
    The **My balance** card shows how many days are available, used and pending. Next to it you see your nearest absence and **Quick actions**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/en/dashboard-employee.webp" alt="Employee dashboard: the My balance card with 14 days available, the Nearest absence card and Quick actions with a New request button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your first request">
    Click **New request**. See [Request an absence](/en/time-off/request-absence).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to find common tasks

| You want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| check your balance | **Dashboard** → **My balance** · [Balances](/en/time-off/balances) |
| submit a request | **New request** · [Request an absence](/en/time-off/request-absence) |
| check the status of your requests | **Requests** → the **Mine** tab |
| cancel a request | [Cancel a request](/en/time-off/cancel-request) |
| see your team's absences | **Calendar** · [Absence calendar](/en/calendars/absence-calendar) |
| add absences to your own calendar app | **Calendar** → **Subscribe (iCal)** |
| change notification preferences | **Settings → My account** · [Sign-in and account](/en/account/sign-in-and-account) |
| file a request about your data | **Settings → Privacy** · [Privacy](/en/account/privacy) |
