Everyone with access to Tivero has one role in a given workspace. The role decides what the person sees in the menu and what they can do.

## Roles

| Role | For whom | In short |
|---|---|---|
| **Employee** | Everyone in the organization | Submits requests, sees their own balances and the calendar. |
| **Manager** | Team supervisors | Approves requests and sees their team's absences. |
| **HR** | The HR department | Manages people, policies, limits and absence types. |
| **Administrator** | People who run Tivero | Manages workspace settings, people, policies, integrations and the plan. |
| **Owner** | The person who created the workspace (or took over ownership) | Full access, including ownership transfer and deleting the organization. |

## What each role sees

| Section | Employee | Manager | HR | Administrator | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard, Calendar, Requests, Settings | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| People, Teams, Reports | — | own team only, read-only | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Absence policies, Absence types | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Integrations | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Plan and billing | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |

## Key permissions

| Action | Employee | Manager | HR | Administrator | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Submit own requests | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Approve requests | when named as the approver | when named as the approver (usually for their own team) | when named as the approver | ✓ | ✓ |
| Reassign an approval to someone else | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Add people, import, balances | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Policies, absence types, schedules, company days | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Data export | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Teams (create and edit) | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Change a member's role | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Integrations, organization settings, plan | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Transfer ownership, delete the organization | — | — | — | — | ✓ |

<Note>
  A request is decided by the approver assigned to the current stage, whatever their role, and also by Administrators and the Owner. Nobody approves their own request. Details: [How approval works](/en/time-off/approval-process).
</Note>

## Change a person's role

**Who:** Administrator or Owner.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person">
    Go to **People** and open the person for editing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the access role">
    Change the access role and save. If the person hasn't accepted their invitation yet, the role is granted when they do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Settings → Members and roles** tab shows how many people have each role, and what the roles mean.

<Warning>
  The Owner role can't be assigned in the person dialog. The Owner can **transfer ownership** to another active member in **Settings → Members and roles**. After the transfer, the previous owner becomes an Administrator. To confirm, you type the new owner's email address. You can't undo this on your own.
</Warning>
