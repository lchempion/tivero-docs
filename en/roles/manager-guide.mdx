**Who:** people with the Manager role, and anyone named as an approver.

## What you see as a manager

- **Dashboard** — the **Requests to review** and **Upcoming team absences** cards.
- **Requests** — the **Mine**, **Team** and **To review** tabs.
- **Calendar** — the **My team** view.
- **People**, **Teams**, **Reports** — your team only, read-only.

## Decide on a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    On the **Dashboard**, click **Review** next to a request, or open **Requests → To review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the decision context">
    The **Decision context** section shows team availability for the period and whether your decision is final or the request still needs another approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Click **Approve** or **Decline**. When you decline, you can add a comment — the requester will see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Details: [Approve requests](/en/time-off/approve-requests).

## Other ways to reach requests

- The email notification about a new request links to the **To review** list in Tivero.
- You can decide directly in Microsoft Teams, if your organization has [turned on Teams notifications](/en/microsoft-365/teams).

## When the manager is away

If you're going away, ask HR to set up [approval delegation](/en/time-off/approval-delegation) for the time you're out.
