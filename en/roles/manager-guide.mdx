**Who:** people with the Manager role, and anyone named as an approver. Detailed instructions are in the linked articles.

## What you do in Tivero

- Decide on requests from the people you approve for.
- Check team availability before you decide.
- Plan work using the team calendar.
- Submit your own requests — like every employee.

## What you see as a manager

- **Dashboard** — the **Requests to review** and **Upcoming team absences** cards.
- **Requests** — the **Mine**, **Team** and **To review** tabs.
- **Calendar** — the **My team** view.
- **People**, **Teams**, **Reports** — your team only, read-only.

## Decide on a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    On the **Dashboard**, click **Review** next to a request, or open **Requests → To review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the decision context">
    The **Decision context** section shows team availability for the period and whether your decision is final or the request still needs another approval.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/en/request-decision.webp" alt="A pending request with the Decision context section: Team available, Your decision is final, and the Decline and Approve buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Click **Approve** or **Decline**. When you decline, you can add a comment — the requester will see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Details: [Approve requests](/en/time-off/approve-requests).

## Where to find common tasks

| You want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| see who will be away | **Calendar** → **My team** · [Absence calendar](/en/calendars/absence-calendar) |
| check a team member's balance | **People** (read-only) · [Balances](/en/time-off/balances) |
| see team statistics | **Reports** (scope: your team) · [Reports](/en/reports/reports) |
| decide on requests in Microsoft Teams | [Microsoft Teams](/en/microsoft-365/teams) — if your organization has turned it on |
| hand approvals over while you're away | **Settings → Approval delegation** · [Approval delegation](/en/time-off/approval-delegation) |

<Note>
  The email notification about a new request links to the **To review** list in Tivero. You make the decision itself in Tivero or in Microsoft Teams.
</Note>
