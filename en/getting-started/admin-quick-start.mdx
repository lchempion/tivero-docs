This article takes you from a new workspace to the first request.

**Who:** the Owner or an Administrator. The HR role can do some of the steps too.
**Prerequisites:** you have created a [workspace](/en/getting-started/create-workspace).

## What Tivero sets up on its own

If your company operates in Poland, Tivero uses your answers on **Let’s tailor Tivero** to set up right away:

- the organization details and the work week,
- the first team, with you as its manager,
- absence policies that match who works with you,
- your policy and opening balance,
- public holidays managed by Tivero for this year and next — you don't need to confirm them,
- your own requests without approval (change this in **People**).

Don't set these up again. You can review and change them in **Settings**, **Teams** and **Absence policies**.

If your company operates in another country, you choose the policies in the wizard and confirm the holiday calendar yourself — see [Holiday calendar](/en/calendars/holiday-calendar).

## The "Company setup" list

In the side menu, the Owner and Administrators see the **Company setup** list with progress, for example "4 of 5". The list checks, as you go, what is still missing before people can submit requests:

| Item | When it's done |
|---|---|
| **Add your people** | The workspace has at least one person besides the Owner. |
| **Set teams and supervisors** | Everyone except the Owner has a supervisor. |
| **Configure absence policies** | Everyone has a policy assigned. |
| **Set opening balances** | People whose policy uses a balance have an opening balance. |
| **Check approval routing** | Everyone whose requests need approval has an approver. |
| **Prepare working days and holidays** | Working days can be calculated for everyone. |

Items that don't apply to your company aren't shown. Click an item to go to the place where you complete it.

When everything is done, the list changes to **Company setup complete** with the suggestion **Try the whole flow — submit your first request**. After the first request is submitted, the list disappears.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Open **People** and click **Add person** or **Import**. When you add a person, Tivero suggests a team, a supervisor, a policy and an opening balance. See [Add and invite people](/en/people/add-and-invite-people).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete what the list shows">
    Work through any remaining items on the **Company setup** list — for example supervisors in [Teams](/en/people/teams-and-supervisors) or [approvals](/en/time-off/approval-process).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitations">
    Invite people you added or imported without an invitation with **Send invitation** on the **People** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the first request">
    When you see **Company setup complete**, click **Submit the first request** and try the whole flow on your own request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Employees sign in, see their balance on the **Dashboard** and submit requests. Requests reach the right approvers, and approved absences appear in the **Calendar**.
