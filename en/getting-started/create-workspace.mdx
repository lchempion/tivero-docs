title: "Create a workspace"
description: "Sign up, answer a few questions about your company and let Tivero prepare a basic setup."
---

A workspace is your organization in Tivero. The person who creates it becomes its **Owner**.

**Who:** the person rolling out Tivero in the company.
**What you need:** an email address and a few minutes. No payment card is needed — a new workspace starts with a free trial.

<Warning>
  If your company already uses Tivero, don't create a new workspace. Ask an administrator in your organization for an invitation. When your address is in the company directory but nobody has invited you yet, Tivero shows **Your organisation already uses Tivero**.
</Warning>

## Sign up

Open [dash.tivero.app/en/sign-up](https://dash.tivero.app/en/sign-up) and register with your email address. Creating an account on its own is not a contract. After you verify your address, Tivero asks a few questions about your company. For a company operating in Poland, Tivero sets it up right away and summarizes it on a third screen; for other countries, you finish the setup in the wizard.

## 1. Your company

On **Let’s start with your company**, enter:

- **First name** and **Last name** — this is how your colleagues will see you in Tivero;
- **Company name**.

Accept the Terms of Service, which incorporate the Data Processing Agreement. You accept them on behalf of the organization. Click **Continue**.

<Note>
  The workspace gets the language you create it in. Switch it with the **Polski** link on this screen, and later in **Settings → Organization → Default language**.
</Note>

## 2. Let’s tailor Tivero

On **Let’s tailor Tivero**, you answer a few questions and Tivero sets up the rest for you:

- **Where your company operates**;
- **Who works with you?** — **Employees**, **B2B contractors** or **Both**. With **Both**, Tivero also asks how you work yourself;
- if you work as a B2B contractor — **Your planned unavailability allowance**: **No limit — just record unavailability** or **Limit** with a number of days. No answer is preselected;
- **Create your first team** — type a name or pick a suggestion, for example **Whole company**.

The **We’ll set up for you** section shows what Tivero prepares for a company in Poland: the work week, the time zone (detected from your browser), public holidays managed by Tivero, absence policies, your policy and opening balance, you as the team manager, and that your requests don’t need approval. Items you can change right away have a **Change** button. If you already used part of your leave before starting with Tivero, change your opening balance.

Click **Set up Tivero**.

<Note>
  If you choose a country other than Poland, Tivero saves your answers and you set up absence policies and the holiday calendar in the next steps of the wizard: you choose policy templates, add people, and then confirm the [holiday calendar](/en/calendars/holiday-calendar).
</Note>

## 3. Tivero is ready (companies in Poland)

The **Tivero is ready for …** screen summarizes what was saved: the country and time zone, the work week, the public holidays managed by Tivero for this year and next, the team, the absence policies, your policy and your opening balance. You can change everything later.

Click **Go to Tivero** to open the **Dashboard**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"We can’t finish setting up Tivero right now. Your data is safe. Please try again shortly."** — Tivero can't prepare the setup at the moment. Your answers are saved; try again later.
- **"Setup is in progress… Refresh the page in a moment."** — the setup is already running. Refresh the page.
- **Finish setting up in Tivero** — the workspace already has settings Tivero didn't create, so the automatic setup doesn't finish. Click **Go to Tivero** and complete the settings using the **Company setup** list.

## What's next

In the side menu, the Owner and Administrators see the **Company setup** list with what's left to do — usually adding people. See the [Admin quick start](/en/getting-started/admin-quick-start).

<Note>
  One account can create one workspace itself. You join other organizations through invitations. If you need another workspace, write to [hello@tivero.app](mailto:hello@tivero.app).
</Note>
