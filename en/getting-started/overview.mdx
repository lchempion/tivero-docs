title: "What is Tivero"
description: "Tivero helps teams plan absences and planned unavailability: requests, approvals, balances and a working-day calendar in one place."
---

Tivero is an app for managing employee absences and the planned unavailability of B2B contractors. An employee submits a request, the right person approves it, and the balance and the team calendar update on their own.

You open the app at [dash.tivero.app](https://dash.tivero.app).

## What you can do in Tivero

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Submit and approve requests" icon="paper-plane" href="/en/time-off/request-absence">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Track balances" icon="scale-balanced" href="/en/time-off/balances">

  <Card title="Count working days" icon="calendar-days" href="/en/calendars/how-working-days-are-counted">

  </Card>

## Core concepts

| Concept | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Your organization in Tivero. Data in one workspace is never visible in another. |
| **Absence type** | The kind of absence, for example "Vacation leave" or "Planned unavailability". |
| **Absence policy** | Rules for a group of people: which types it covers, the limit, and how approval works. |
| **Balance** | The days or hours available to a person under a policy. |
| **Approval** | The approver's decision: the request is approved or declined. |

Read more in [Key concepts](/en/time-off/key-concepts).

## Languages

Tivero is available in Polish and English. You pick the language when you create the workspace, and you can change the organization's default in **Settings → Organization → Default language**.
