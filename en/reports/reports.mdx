**Who:** HR, Administrator, Owner — the whole organization. Managers — their own team only.
**Where:** the **Organization → Reports** menu.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Range** | **This month**, **This quarter**, **This year** or **Custom range** |
| **Team** | one or more teams |
| **Engagement type** | Employment, B2B, Mandate contract |
| **Request status** | Pending, Approved, Declined, Cancelled |

Click **Clear** to remove the filters.

## What the report shows

**Metrics**

- **Absent today**
- **Pending requests**
- **Days used this year**
- **People without a policy** — if anyone has no policy, the metric shows **Needs attention**.

**Sections**

- **Team coverage** — for each team: people, absent today, upcoming and pending absences.
- **Limit utilization** — for each policy: days used and days remaining.
- **Recent requests**.

## Export from the report

In the **Exports** section, choose an export and click **Export**:

| Export | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Requests export** | Absence requests with their statuses. |
| **Balances export** | Balances and limit utilization for the current year. |
| **Team absences export** | Absence summary broken down by team. |
| **CSV export** | Raw data for further spreadsheet analysis. |

Exports respect the selected range and filters. Managers export only their own team's data (**Scope: Your team**).
