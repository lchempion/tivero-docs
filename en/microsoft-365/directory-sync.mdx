---
title: "Sync people from Entra ID"
description: "Import and update people in Tivero from the Microsoft Entra ID directory, with a preview of changes before anything is saved."
---

**Who:** Administrator or Owner.
**Prerequisites:** a [connected Microsoft 365 integration](/en/microsoft-365/connect) and the Growth or Enterprise plan (or the trial).
**Where:** **Integrations → Microsoft 365 → People — Microsoft Entra ID**.

## First sync

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare a preview">
    Click **Preview changes**. Tivero compares the directory with the people in Tivero. Nothing is saved yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the change plan">
    The preview groups people into **New people**, **To update**, **Deactivation candidates**, **Conflicts** and **No change**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Apply** to save the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** new people appear on the **People** list with the **Imported** status. Tivero **doesn't send** them invitations automatically — invite them when they're ready. See [Add and invite people](/en/people/add-and-invite-people).

Later, you can run a sync manually with **Sync now**.

## What data is synced

Email address, first name, last name, display name, job title, department, account status and — if you turn on manager sync — the supervisor. Tivero only reads the directory; it never changes anything in it.

## Sync settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Automatic synchronization** | Automatic directory synchronization **is not available at the moment**. To fetch current people data, use **Sync now**. |
| **Manager synchronization** | Sets the supervisor from the directory, which feeds the [approval route](/en/time-off/approval-process). Never clears an existing supervisor. |
| **Automatic offboarding** | **Not available at the moment.** Tivero doesn't deactivate anyone automatically — you deactivate deactivation candidates manually. |

## Deactivation candidates

People who have gone from the directory or whose account is disabled end up in **Deactivation candidates**. They are not deactivated automatically — HR deactivates them manually on the **People** list.

<Note>
  Scheduled sync and automatic offboarding are not available at the moment. You fetch current directory data with **Sync now**, and a person always decides on deactivation.
</Note>

## Conflicts

A conflict means Tivero can't unambiguously match a person in the directory to a person in Tivero. Resolve the conflict in Tivero or in the directory and run the sync again.
