title: "Disconnect the integration"
description: "What happens to people, calendars and the Teams app after you disconnect Microsoft 365."
---

**Who:** Administrator or Owner.
**Where:** **Integrations → Microsoft 365 → Manage integration**.

## Disconnect the integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open management">
    Open **Microsoft 365** on the **Integrations** page and go to the **Manage integration** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect">
    Click **Disconnect integration** and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes and what stays

| Item | After disconnecting |
|---|---|
| People sync | Stopped. |
| Outlook calendar sync | Stopped. |
| Teams notifications | Stopped. |
| People and data in Tivero | Unchanged. |
| Existing Outlook events | Stay. |
| The Tivero app in Teams | Not removed automatically. A Teams administrator can remove it in Teams Admin Center. |

<Tip>
  If you only want to stop sending notifications to Teams or adding events to Outlook, turn off the relevant toggle instead of disconnecting the whole integration.
</Tip>

To connect again, follow [Connect Microsoft 365](/en/microsoft-365/connect).
