## Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Role in Tivero** | Integrations are set up by an Administrator or the Owner. |
| **Tivero plan** | Outlook calendar sync — from the Start plan. People import, automatic directory sync and Microsoft Teams — the Growth or Enterprise plan. During the trial, all Growth features are available. |
| **Microsoft 365** | A Microsoft 365 administrator in your organization to grant consent. Publishing the app in Teams needs a Teams administrator. |

## What Tivero does and doesn't do

- Tivero **reads** data from your Microsoft 365 directory. It never writes anything to it.
- In calendars, Tivero creates and removes only the events for its own absences.
- The Tivero app for Teams isn't available in the Microsoft Teams store. An administrator publishes it in your organization's app catalog.
