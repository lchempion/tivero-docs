**Who manages the plan:** the Owner and Administrators.
**Where:** **Settings → Plan & billing**.

## Free trial

- A new workspace gets **30 days** of the full Growth plan.
- No payment card is required.
- You can choose a plan at any point during the trial. You pay nothing today — the first payment happens when the trial ends.
- If you don't choose a plan, after 30 days the workspace becomes **read-only**. Your data stays, you can export it, and new changes wait for a plan.

## Founding Partners

Founding Partners is an invitation-only programme for our first customers.

- The trial lasts **60 days** instead of 30.
- The Growth plan costs **50% less for the first 12 paid months**, then the standard Growth price applies.
- The discount applies to **monthly billing** of the Growth plan only.
- You can buy the discounted Growth plan **during the trial or after it ends**, as long as the first paid period starts no later than **30 May 2027**. Buying during the trial does not shorten it — you pay from its end. Buying after the trial starts the paid subscription straight away, with no new trial.
- The discount is granted once. After 30 May 2027, or once the discount has been used, plans are at the standard price.
- If you change from Growth to Start, the change takes effect from the next renewal and the discount **ends permanently** on that date. Returning to Growth later does not restore the discount.

The full rules are in the Programme Terms, which you accept before you buy.

You pay for Start and Growth monthly or yearly. You see the current prices in **Plan & billing** and on the [pricing page](https://tivero.app/en/pricing). For Enterprise, click **Let's talk**.

## Who counts as a billable person

We bill **active** people — those who have accepted their invitation or been activated manually. You don't buy seats up front.

- Adding a profile or sending an invitation costs nothing on its own.
- Deactivated, archived and deleted people aren't billed.
- We bill a minimum of 10 seats, even when fewer people are active.

When activating another person would raise the number of billable seats, Tivero shows the cost and asks you to confirm before anything is charged.

## Who can buy Tivero

At launch, Tivero sells only to businesses in the European Union. The price and currency follow the billing country.
