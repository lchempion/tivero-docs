**Who:** the Owner and Administrators.
**Where:** **Settings → Plan & billing**.

## Buy a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the billing country">
    The price and currency follow the billing country. If you're billed from a different country than the one suggested, change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your company details">
    Enter the company name, EU VAT number (with the country prefix) and address. Without these you can't choose a plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the plan and cycle">
    Choose a plan (Start or Growth) and the **Billing cycle** (monthly or yearly). Accept the Terms of Service, including the Data Processing Agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Enter a payment method in the payment provider's form. Card details never reach Tivero's servers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** the plan starts once the payment provider confirms the payment. This can take a moment — Tivero then shows **We are confirming your payment with the provider**. If you buy during the trial, you pay nothing today and the first payment happens when the trial ends.

## Change your plan

| Change | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **To a bigger plan** | Immediately. Tivero shows the amount due for the current period, and the new features switch on once the payment is confirmed. |
| **To a smaller plan** | From the next billing period. Tivero lists the features you use that will stop being available. No data is deleted. Until it takes effect, you can click **Undo the scheduled change**. |

### Status of a scheduled change

A scheduled change of your plan or number of seats appears in **Scheduled changes**. Tivero shows there whether the payment provider has confirmed it:

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| The change with its date | The payment provider has confirmed the change and will apply it on that date. |
| **We are waiting for the payment provider to confirm this change.** | Tivero saved the change, but the payment provider has not confirmed it yet. Until it does, your current terms apply. Check again later. |
| **Our team is reviewing this change — it has not been scheduled yet.** | The change could not be scheduled automatically. Until it is resolved, your current terms apply. Don't choose it again. If you have questions, write to us at [hello@tivero.app](mailto:hello@tivero.app). |

After you choose a change, Tivero tells you what actually happened:

- **Change saved. We are still confirming it with the payment provider** — the change is saved but not confirmed yet. You can follow it in **Scheduled changes**.
- **This change could not be scheduled and nothing was changed** — your plan and terms stay as they were. You can try again later.
- **We cannot confirm this change yet** — don't choose it again. If you have questions, write to us at [hello@tivero.app](mailto:hello@tivero.app).

**Undo the scheduled change** only undoes a change that can actually be withdrawn. If it can't, Tivero tells you that the change was not undone and is still saved. You can try again in a moment.

## Seats

- An increase in seats (for example inviting someone beyond your paid seats) applies immediately, prorated for the current period. You always see the amount before you confirm.
- A decrease in seats (for example deactivating someone) applies from the next period, once the payment provider confirms it. We don't refund part of a started period.
- A lower number of seats that is not confirmed yet doesn't stop you from deactivating someone. Tivero tells you about it after the deactivation. Until it is confirmed, the number of billed seats may stay the same. You can check it in **Settings → Plan & billing**, under **Scheduled changes**.

## Payment method and invoice details

- Click **Change payment method** to open the payment provider's portal and change your card.
- With an active subscription, change the company name, address and VAT number on future invoices with **Update invoice details**.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** section lists your invoices. You can open each one and download it as a **PDF**.

## Cancel your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    In the **Cancel subscription** section, click **Cancel subscription** and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it until the period ends">
    You keep full access until the end of the paid period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**After the period ends**, the workspace becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and data export remains available. Until the end date, you can click **Restore subscription**.

If you cancel during the trial, you pay nothing and the trial still runs to the end.

## Failed payment

If a payment fails, nothing changes for the first week — update your payment method. After that, the workspace becomes read-only. Your data is untouched. See [Billing issues](/en/troubleshooting/billing-issues).
