## I can't choose a plan

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Confirm your billing country to choose a plan." | Confirm the billing country in **Plan and billing**. |
| "Complete your company details (name, VAT number, address) to choose a plan." | Complete your company details. |
| "At launch Tivero sells only to businesses in the European Union." | Tivero can currently be bought only by EU businesses. |
| "We couldn't confirm your EU VAT number." | Check the VAT number (with the country prefix) and the billing country. |
| "Your EU VAT number is still being verified." | Wait a moment and try again. |
| "A payment is in progress." | Refresh the page in a moment. |
| I don't see **Plan and billing** | Only the Owner and Administrators manage the plan. |

## A payment failed

Tivero shows: "The last payment failed."

1. Click **Change payment method** or **Update payment method**.
2. Update your card in the payment provider's portal.

Nothing changes for the first week. If the payment still isn't settled, the workspace becomes read-only.

## The workspace is read-only

| Cause | How to restore full access |
|---|---|
| The trial ended | Choose a plan in **Plan and billing**. |
| Unpaid invoice | Update your payment method. |
| The subscription ended | Choose a plan again. |

In read-only mode your data is safe and you can export it, but new changes (for example requests) are blocked.

## The payment was interrupted

If you close the payment form before finishing, you see "The payment was interrupted." You can try again.

## I can't open the payment form

If Tivero says the problem was reported to the Tivero team automatically, retrying won't fix it. Wait and try later, or write to [hello@tivero.app](mailto:hello@tivero.app).
