title: "Holiday calendar needs setup"
description: "What to do when a request shows \"The holiday calendar needs to be set up by an administrator.\""
---

## Symptom

When you submit a request, you see:

> The holiday calendar needs to be set up by an administrator.

Administrators also see a **Set up the calendar** link.

## Cause

Tivero counts requests only from a holiday calendar your organization has **confirmed**. For the person's work location and the year of the request, the calendar has the **Needs confirmation** status. This is a deliberate safeguard — Tivero doesn't guess which days are off for you.

The most common situations:

- a new workspace where nobody has confirmed the calendar yet;
- a request for next year, whose calendar hasn't been confirmed yet;
- the person was given a new [work location](/en/calendars/work-locations) in another country.

## Fix

**Employee:** ask an administrator or HR to confirm the calendar. Give them the year and dates of your request.

**HR, Administrator, Owner:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the calendar">
    Click **Set up the calendar**, or go to **Settings → Work time → Holidays**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the work location and year">
    Choose the person's work location and the year of the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the holidays, correct them if needed and click **Confirm the … calendar**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** the person can submit the request right after you confirm.

## "No data" status

If the calendar has the **No data** status, Tivero doesn't have a calendar for this work location and year yet, so requests for that period can't be calculated yet. Write to us at [hello@tivero.app](mailto:hello@tivero.app).
