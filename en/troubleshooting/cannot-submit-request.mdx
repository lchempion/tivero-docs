---
title: "I can't submit a request"
description: "What the messages that block a request mean, and who can clear them."
---

When a request can't be submitted, Tivero shows a message with the reason. Find it in the table below.

| Message | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "The holiday calendar needs to be set up by an administrator." | The holiday calendar for your work location and year isn't confirmed. | See [Holiday calendar needs setup](/en/troubleshooting/holiday-calendar-needs-setup). |
| "We can't calculate working days correctly for this work location and period right now." | There's no calendar data for this period, or no work schedule. | An administrator checks the [holiday calendar](/en/calendars/holiday-calendar) (status **No data**) and the person's [work schedule](/en/calendars/work-schedules). |
| "The opening balance has not been set yet." | The policy requires a balance, but no opening balance is set. | HR sets the balance on the person page. See [Balances](/en/time-off/balances). |
| "Your allowance has not been set yet." | The policy uses a **Set individually** allowance, and yours isn't set. | HR sets the allowance under **Individual settings**. |
| "…you have no absence rules assigned yet." | You don't have an absence policy. | HR [assigns a policy](/en/time-off/absence-policies). |
| "Your absence rules are not configured yet." | The policy is incomplete. | HR completes the policy (types and allowance). |
| "This absence type needs a pool that has not been configured yet." | The type draws on a pool the policy doesn't define. | HR sets the pool allowance in the policy. |
| "This absence type is not available for your form of collaboration." | The chosen type doesn't match your collaboration form. | Choose another type, or ask HR to check your collaboration form and policy. |
| "No one is configured to approve this request." | No approver can be determined. | See [Approval issues](/en/troubleshooting/approval-issues). |
| "The selected range contains no working days." | The range covers only days off or holidays. | Check the dates. See [How working days are counted](/en/calendars/how-working-days-are-counted). |

## The absence type isn't on the list

The list shows only types your policy covers that are active in the organization. Ask HR to check the [policy](/en/time-off/absence-policies) and the [type](/en/time-off/absence-types).

## The request is longer or shorter than you expect

Open the request and check the **Calculation details** section (approvers: the **How was … calculated?** link). If a day is marked wrong, an administrator corrects the [holiday calendar](/en/calendars/holiday-calendar), [company days](/en/calendars/company-days) or [work schedule](/en/calendars/work-schedules).

## The workspace is read-only

After the trial ends without a plan, or with an unpaid invoice, new requests are blocked. See [Billing issues](/en/troubleshooting/billing-issues).
