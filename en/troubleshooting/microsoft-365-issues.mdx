## Connection

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| Status **Needs reconnection** | Microsoft revoked or invalidated access. Click **Reconnect** and sign in with a Microsoft 365 administrator account. |
| The IT authorization link doesn't work | The link expires after 72 hours. Click **Regenerate** and share the new link. |
| Status **Needs confirmation** | Your IT admin authorized access. Check the organization and click **Confirm and activate**. |
| The wrong Microsoft 365 organization was authorized | Click **Reject**. The integration is disconnected and you can connect again. |
| **Available on the Growth plan** message | Directory sync requires the Growth or Enterprise plan. See [Plans and trial](/en/billing/plans-and-trial). |

## People sync

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| Synced people didn't get invitations | This is intended — people from the directory are **Imported**. Invite them with **Send invitation**. |
| Someone who left is still active | Deactivation candidates aren't deactivated automatically. Deactivate them manually on the **People** list. |
| **Automatic offboarding** can't be turned on | It requires several consecutive complete syncs on the same scope. Keep syncing until Tivero lets you turn it on. |
| **Suspended by the circuit breaker** | The sync detected a sudden spike of deactivation candidates and deactivated nobody. Review the scope in the directory, then turn the feature on again. |
| The supervisor isn't set | Turn on **Manager synchronization**. Tivero never clears an existing supervisor. |

## Outlook calendar

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| An absence doesn't appear in Outlook | Wait a few minutes after approval. Check that the person is synced from the directory and that syncing is on. |
| Tivero shows people who need attention | Events couldn't be created for these people. Check that they have an active account and mailbox in Microsoft 365. |
| An event didn't disappear after syncing was turned off | This is intended — existing events stay. They disappear only when an approved request is cancelled. |
| A partial-day absence isn't in Outlook | Partial-day absences aren't synced. |

## Microsoft Teams

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Microsoft Teams administrator consent required** | A Microsoft 365 administrator clicks **Grant administrator consent**. |
| **Publish the Tivero app in Microsoft Teams** | A Teams administrator uploads the package: Teams Admin Center → **Teams apps → Manage apps → Upload new app**. |
| The test notification doesn't arrive | Check that the Tivero app is available in your organization. After publishing, installation can take a moment. |
| **Some people cannot receive notifications** | Check that these people have a Microsoft Teams licence and an active account. |
| A Teams tab asks to finish setup | An administrator clicks **Set up Teams tabs** in Tivero. If the prompt doesn't go away, click **Run setup again**. |
| Teams runs an older version of the app | Download the current package and upload it in Teams Admin Center through **Manage apps → Tivero → Update**. |
