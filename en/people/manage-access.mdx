**Who:** HR, Administrator, Owner.

## The person page

Click a person on the **People** list to open their page. There you find:

| Section | What you set there |
|---|---|
| **Balance** | The opening balance, adjustments, carry-over to the next year and balance history. See [Balances](/en/time-off/balances). |
| **Individual settings** | A person-specific limit for a chosen absence type, independent of the policy (**Individual dimension**). |
| **Work location** | The work location country and the date it applies from. It decides the person's [holiday calendar](/en/calendars/work-locations). |
| **Work schedule** | An individual work week. See [Work schedules](/en/calendars/work-schedules). |
| **Approval routing** | Who approves this person's requests: the organization settings, the team settings or people named individually. |

## Individual dimension

An individual dimension is a person's own limit for a chosen absence type. From the date you choose, it applies instead of the policy's limit.

The dimension states what the person is entitled to, but it doesn't change their balance. Requests are settled against the [balance](/en/time-off/balances), which you set in the **Balance** section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person">
    Click the person on the **People** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dimension">
    On the **Absences** tab, in **Individual settings**, click **Add setting** (or **Manage** if the person already has individual settings). In the **Individual dimension** dialog, choose the **Absence type**, enter the **Limit** or select **No limit (B2B)**, and set **Effective from**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### A limit of 0 and "No limit"

- Entering **0** sets the dimension to zero. It doesn't remove days the person already has in their balance. For a type that reduces the balance, the person can't submit a request until you set their balance.
- **No limit (B2B)** means there is no limit.
- An empty field isn't saved. Tivero asks for a valid limit. A limit can have at most two decimal places.

### Changing the dimension from a date

A new dimension replaces the current one from its **Effective from** date. The previous period isn't removed: it ends the day before. The person page shows the dimension in force and any scheduled dimensions.

Example: a person has 20 days from 1 January 2026. You save 26 days from 1 July 2026. From 1 January to 30 June, 20 days apply. From 1 July, 26 days apply.

Changing the dimension doesn't change the person's balance. Saving the same dimension with the same date again changes nothing.

### When Tivero refuses to save

Tivero doesn't guess which period should apply. The save is refused and the current settings stay unchanged when:

- another dimension of this type already starts on the same day,
- a dimension of this type is scheduled from a later date,
- the dimension in force has its own end date,
- the person has several dimensions of this type in force at the same time.

Check the person's individual dimensions on their page and choose a date that doesn't conflict with an existing period.

### When several dimensions cover the same period

Older settings can contain several dimensions of the same type in force at once. The person page then shows a **Needs review** warning:

- **Applied for now**: the dimension Tivero is using at the moment, based on its precedence rule. This doesn't mean the value is correct.
- **To review**: the other dimensions covering that period.
- **Scheduled · to review**: a dimension that will overlap another one once it starts.

Tivero doesn't correct anything by itself or suggest the right limit. Check the values and dates before making further changes.

## Deactivate a person

Deactivate someone who is leaving the organization or should no longer have access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person">
    Click the person on the **People** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deactivate">
    Click **Deactivate** and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** the person loses access to Tivero. Their data, requests and balances are unchanged. On a paid plan, a deactivated person doesn't count as a paid seat.

<Warning>
  The only owner of a workspace can't be deactivated. [Transfer ownership](/en/roles/roles-and-permissions) to someone else first.
</Warning>

## Reactivate a person

Open a person with the **Deactivated** status and click **Reactivate**. If reactivating increases the number of paid seats, Tivero asks you to confirm the cost.

## Access role

You change the role in the person's edit dialog. See [Roles and permissions](/en/roles/roles-and-permissions).
