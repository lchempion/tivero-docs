---
title: "Add and invite people"
description: "Add a person to the organization, review the settings Tivero prepares automatically, and send the invitation right away."
---

**Who:** HR, Administrator, Owner.
**Prerequisites:** at least one [absence policy](/en/time-off/absence-policies), if you want to assign it right away.

When you add a person, you type only their first name, last name and email address. Tivero prepares the rest of the setup from your company's settings and shows it to you before anything is saved. By default, the person is invited as soon as they are added.

## Add a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Go to **People** and click **Add person**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the person's details">
    Enter the **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/en/add-person.webp" alt="The Add person dialog: First name, Last name and Email fields, the employment type choice, and the We'll set up automatically section with employment type, team, manager, policy, starting balance, role and invitation, followed by Change settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions, if any">
    Tivero asks only about what it can't safely decide on its own:

    - **This person's employment type** — when your company works with people on different employment types, or the type isn't known. No answer is preselected.
    - **Planned unavailability allowance** — for a person on B2B collaboration, when their policy leaves the allowance to be set per person. Choose **No limit**, or **Limit** and enter the **Number of days**. No answer is preselected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review We'll set up automatically">
    The section shows what Tivero will set up: **Employment type**, **Team**, **Manager**, **Policy**, **Starting balance**, **Role** (Employee by default) and **Invitation**. **To be chosen** means you need to pick that value in the settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change settings if needed">
    Click **Change settings** to choose other values: **Collaboration form**, **Access role** (for Administrators and Owners), **Team**, **Supervisor**, **Policy**, **Opening balance** with the **Leave year** field, and **Send the invitation now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add and invite**. If you turn off **Send the invitation now**, the button is called **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After saving, the person appears on the **People** list. If the invitation was sent, their status is **Invited**. If they were added without an invitation, their status is **Imported**.

<Warning>
  The email address is the account key and can't be edited later. Check it before you save.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The **Supervisor** defines the reporting line and, by default, approves the person's requests. The **Access role** defines permissions in Tivero. The two are independent.
</Note>

## Starting balance

If the policy uses a balance, Tivero suggests a starting balance equal to the policy's full allowance for the current leave year. Change it if the employee already used part of their leave before starting with Tivero. Tivero doesn't prorate the allowance — when the person starts mid-year, the dialog reminds you: **This person starts mid-year — check the allowance.**

If Tivero can't safely suggest an amount, the field is empty and you need to fill it in.

### B2B collaboration and No limit

“No limit” applies to the allowance. Planned unavailability may still be accounted against a balance, so Tivero can require a starting balance. Tivero doesn't suggest an amount for **No limit** — enter it in the **Opening balance** field.

## If something doesn't work

- **The invitation wasn't sent.** Tivero tells you the person was added, but the invitation could not be sent. The person and their setup are saved. Click **Send again** — don't add the person a second time.
- **The setup wasn't finished.** If, for example, the policy or the balance couldn't be set, Tivero tells you which part is missing. The person is added, but no invitation is sent. Click **Open profile**, complete the missing settings, then send the invitation.

## Send the invitation later

This is how you invite a person added without an invitation, or imported from a file:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person">
    Click the person on the **People** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite">
    Click **Send invitation**. The status changes to **Invited**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for acceptance">
    The person receives an invitation email. Once they accept it and sign in, the status changes to **Active** and they get the access role you chose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Seat cost

On a paid plan you pay for active people. If adding or inviting a person increases the number of paid seats, Tivero shows **This will increase the bill** before saving, with how much you pay and when. During the free trial you pay nothing. Only the Owner or an Administrator can approve the cost. Details: [Subscription and payments](/en/billing/subscription-and-payments).

## Person statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Imported** | The profile exists; no invitation has been sent yet. |
| **Invited** | The invitation was sent; the person hasn't accepted it yet. |
| **Active** | The person has access to Tivero. |
| **Deactivated** | The person has lost access. Their data is kept. |
